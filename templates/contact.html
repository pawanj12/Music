Before you visit

A few things people usually ask before their first class.

Can I just walk in, or do I need an appointment?
Walk-ins are welcome for a quick chat, but the demo class itself needs a slot booked in advance so a teacher is free for you.
Is parking available?
Yes, there's two-wheeler and car parking right outside the building.
Do I need to bring my own instrument?
No — for the demo class, everything is provided. Once you enrol, we'll guide you on what to buy based on your level.
Is the demo class really free?
Yes, no cost and no obligation to continue after.
